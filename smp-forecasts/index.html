Sources and interpretation

Forecasts come from the RBA SMP forecast archive, supplemented with the November 2026 detailed table. The window includes both endpoint releases: nine SMPs over two years. Each forecast connects to the last available quarter before its first forecast point. GDP and wage releases lag the SMP cut-off, so their starting observations are generally two quarters before the publication quarter. Ended-quarter nowcasts are retained as forecasts. Connecting observations use the displayed actual series, which may contain later revisions; they do not recreate the exact data vintage seen by the RBA and are never scored as forecasts.

Actuals through June 2026 use ABS series republished in RBA statistical tables, downloaded on 27 September 2026: inflation (G1), GDP (H1), unemployment (H5) and wages (H4). September 2026 inflation and unemployment are from the November SMP; September wages are from the ABS November release. Older observations have not been refreshed for subsequent revisions. This is a dated comparison, not a live feed or a first-release forecast evaluation.

Inflation, real GDP and wages are four-quarter growth rates. Unemployment is the seasonally adjusted quarterly average; incomplete quarters are excluded. Inflation uses quarterly measures, not the monthly CPI annual rate. Forecasts are conditional on the RBA’s assumptions, including the market-implied cash-rate path. Misses can reflect shocks, changed assumptions and data revisions, as well as forecasting errors.
