ECX2300

Crashes, crises and macroeconomic policy

Explores major economic disruptions, including the Global Financial Crisis and the COVID-19 recession, through macroeconomic theory and evidence. Topics include liquidity traps, secular stagnation, inequality, monetary policy and automation, with an emphasis on how economic data informs policy decisions.

Monash Handbook

ECC3660

Business cycle fluctuations and monetary policy

Examines how monetary policy interacts with economic activity and financial markets. Students use a New Keynesian framework and empirical evidence to analyse conventional and unconventional policy, assess central-bank responses to deep recessions, and evaluate contemporary monetary-policy debates.
