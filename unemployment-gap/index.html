What the measures mean

Unemployment gap = unemployment − RBA NAIRU assumption. Negative values indicate unemployment below the estimated inflation-stable rate; positive values indicate unemployment above it. Each SMP round is paired with the preceding complete quarter’s seasonally adjusted unemployment average. This is a calculation from the sourced inputs, not an official published RBA gap series.

Judgement adjustment = RBA NAIRU assumption − RBA model average. Negative values mean the RBA assumed a lower NAIRU than its models implied. Models use an older reference quarter than unemployment where national accounts lag; the table reports that quarter. The November 2025 comparison uses the model without CPI nowcasts (4.82%); including nowcasts (4.91%) would give −0.41 percentage points.

The NAIRU is unobserved and uncertain. These points are a record of estimates available in different forecast rounds, not a fixed-method history. Lines only guide the eye between adjacent available observations. No missing estimates are interpolated. The November 2026 assumption is an approximate overall assessment; February 2025 is left blank because a final internal assumption has not been established in this compilation.
