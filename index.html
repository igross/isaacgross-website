About the data

Each frame uses the last available observation in that month. Forward rates extend three years ahead in quarterly steps and retain the RBA’s continuously compounded convention. The actual line is the published cash rate target, which can differ from the overnight market rate at the start of a forward curve. Archived F17 files combine earlier history with a newer release; historical estimates may have been revised. Playback advances through monthly snapshots without inventing intermediate forecasts.
